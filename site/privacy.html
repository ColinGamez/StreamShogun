1. Overview

StreamShōgun ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to collecting only what is necessary to provide and improve the service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. This is used for authentication and subscription management.

Billing Information

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials. We receive only a subscription status and Stripe customer ID.

Cloud Sync Data (Pro only)

If you enable Cloud Sync, your playlist URLs, favorites, and settings are synced to our servers. This data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored in a secured database. We do not inspect or analyze the content of your playlists.

Application Usage

The desktop application operates locally. We do not collect telemetry, analytics, usage statistics, or personal data from the desktop app unless you explicitly enable Cloud Sync or create an account.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track which channels you watch or when you watch them
  • We do not collect IP addresses for analytics or profiling
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing
  • We do not use advertising trackers, pixels, or third-party analytics on our website
  • We do not access or inspect the content of your M3U playlists or XMLTV data

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and manage your subscription
  • To provide Cloud Sync functionality when enabled
  • To send transactional emails (e.g., billing receipts, password resets)
  • To respond to support requests

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured infrastructure. All data in transit is protected with TLS encryption. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We regularly review our security practices.

6. Cookies

Our website uses httpOnly session cookies for authentication. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party cookie-based analytics.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days. Billing records may be retained as required by law.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact ColinKenny@programmer.net.

9. Children's Privacy

StreamShōgun is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at ColinKenny@programmer.net.
